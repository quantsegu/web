Dossier contents

What has to exist on paper

One digital folder, plus a printed set carried in the truck. An inspector asking for the self-control file is not a hypothetical — mobile food businesses are inspected on the same risk basis as fixed premises.

Scope note. This tracker reflects the corrected sequence: the Reisendenbewilligung is included, the city food markets are treated as a separate stall business rather than a truck channel, and the agricultural import licensing question (GEB and tariff quotas) is placed ahead of the veterinary question, which the Switzerland–EU common veterinary area largely settles.

Sources. Kantonales Labor Zürich; Amt für Wirtschaft Kanton Zürich (mobile food stands); BAZG customs regions, tariff rulings and Passar; BLW agricultural import licensing; BLV common veterinary area; Stadt Zürich commercial permits and food markets; German Customs central information service.
